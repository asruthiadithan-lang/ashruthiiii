About me

I am a Master's graduate in Public Policy from St. Xavier's College, Mumbai, with an academic grounding in the social sciences and governance, and experience in research, project coordination, sustainability and field-based work.

My research and analytical interests centre on how policies are designed and, more importantly, how they are carried out on the ground.
